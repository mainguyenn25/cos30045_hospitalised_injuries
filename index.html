Hospitalised Injuries from Road Crashes in Australia

Analyse and visualise trends related to hospitalised injuries from road traffic accidents in Australia, 2011 to 2021

Explore the Context

Problem Statement and Objectives

  1. Background

    Road traffic accidents are a major public health concern in Australia, causing thousands of hospitalisations each year and creating a burden on society.

  2. Motivation

    Although road fatalities receive considerable attention, non-fatal road traffic injuries affect a much larger number of people and impose a burden on the healthcare system.

  3. Audiences

    Policymakers, road safety agencies, researchers, and members of the public who want to explore trends in road traffic injury hospitalisations across Australia.

  4. Purpose of Visualisation

    The aim is to critically analyse and visualise trends related to hospitalised injuries from road traffic accidents in Australia.
